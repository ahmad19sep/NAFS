## App name (30 characters max)

NAFS: Habits, Tasks & Prayer

## Short description (80 characters max)

Plan your day, keep your habits, pray on time and see your progress honestly.

## Full description (4,000 characters max)

NAFS is a calm, honest companion for the person you are becoming. It brings your tasks, habits, prayers, health and goals together in one daily rhythm, and it never pretends a missed day didn't happen.

PLAN YOUR DAY
• Daily, weekly and monthly tasks with due times and priorities
• A reminder before each task is due, with Mark done and Snooze right on the notification
• A morning plan and an evening review to close the day

BUILD HABITS THAT LAST
• Yes/no, counter, duration and reading-progress habits
• Daily, chosen weekdays or a number of times each week
• Streaks that respect your rest days, plus a reminder for each habit if you want one

PRAY ON TIME (OPTIONAL FAITH MODE)
• Log the five daily prayers, alone or in jamaat, plus extra prayers
• Prayer times for your area, or for a city you choose, using common calculation methods and Hanafi Asr
• An alert when each prayer begins, with a Prayed button
• Qibla direction, Hijri date and a Qur'an reading journal
• Faith mode can be turned off at any time without losing your history

LOOK AFTER YOURSELF
• Sleep, water, steps, exercise, weight, mood and meals
• Your own custom health trackers

GOALS AND CHALLENGES
• Goals with milestones that show true progress
• Daily, weekly, monthly or yearly challenges, with optional photo proof and a sadqa pledge

A COACH THAT KNOWS YOUR WEEK
• Talk through a setback and get a small, practical recovery plan. Nothing is added until you choose it.
• Write a letter to your future self and get a reply when the day comes
• Weekly and monthly reports, and a 30-day history, that show unknown days as unknown rather than as failures

STAY ON TRACK
• An end-of-day summary and a Sunday weekly report, if you turn them on
• A home-screen widget and quick shortcuts
• Light and dark themes

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads. No third-party analytics or tracking.
• Your session and sensitive saved data are encrypted on your phone
• Location is optional, approximate, used only while the app is open, and only to calculate prayer times
• Delete your account and all your data from inside the app

NAFS works best with notifications and "Alarms & reminders" allowed, so prayer and task alerts arrive on time.
